SPAI MEMBERSHIP PRIVILEGES

PRIVILEGES

The Sports psychology Association of India (SPAI) offers numerous privileges to its members, which are designed to enhance the growth of sports psychology and provide critical support for athletes and professionals in the field. Membership privileges are:

Professional Networking

Sports Psychology Association of India provides opportunities for members to connect with national and international professionals in sports psychology, through affiliations with organizations like the International Society of Sports Psychology and Asian South-Pacific Association of Sports psychology.

Access to research and resources

Sports Psychology Association of India offers members access to the latest research in sports psychology, including journals and publications. Members also get the opportunity to publish their own work, contributing to the academic body of sports psychology knowledge in India.

Training and Educations

Sports Psychology actively promote sports psychology education in India by supporting the creation of degree courses and programs in sports psychology. Members can benefit from these initiatives by gaining access to specialized training and certification courses.

Awards and Recognition

Sports Psychology Association of India acknowledges the contributions of its members through various awards such as the Best Research paper, Lifetime Achievement Award, and Young Scientist Award.

Discounted Rates for SPAI Events

SPAI members get the privilege of discounted rates in SPAI National Conferences and SPAI seminars and workshops

These privileges position Sports Psychology Association of India to significantly contribute to the growth and development of sports psychology in India, while advancing their own careers through research, education and networking opportunities.
